Pixels

  • Dust, stone, dense and core rock. Gray filler. It gets harder, and pays more, the farther you go from the center.
  • Red explodes when it breaks and can set off the reds around it, until the chain fizzles.
  • Blue is liquid. It is weak and it flows into your tunnels.
  • Green is alive. It regrows into empty space next to it.
  • Gold is hard and rare. It pays for the Precision branch.
  • Glitch drops shards and scrambles the pixels around it into random colors.
  • Static (Spiral Galaxy) jumps away from any hit that does not delete it at once. Hit it hard: bombs, crits, big brushes. It pays shards.
  • Ice (Moon Lake) is tough, but breaking one shatters its whole cluster. Explosions melt it into blue.

The Dead Pixel

Every picture hides one black pixel that cannot be erased. Buried in rock it only flickers now and then. Erase a pixel next to it by hand and it is exposed for a few seconds: click it to delete it for Bits and a recovered note. Bombs, bugs and rigs that open it up make it flee deeper. The Scanner and Pixel Sense in the Bit tree help you find it.

Strategies

  • Demolition runs on red ink. Bombs, chain reactions and payouts from explosions.
  • Precision runs on gold. A tunable laser, clean cuts and drill rigs that work alone.
  • Swarm runs on green. Bugs that tunnel by themselves and multiply your ink.
  • Alchemy runs on blue. The Prism cracks rock and turns gray into colors, and the Transmuter turns any ink into the one you need.
  • Hybrid nodes sit between two branches and reward mixing them.

Defrag, Select All, Restore

Defrag opens at 20% revealed. It wipes the run (ink, upgrades, machines) for Bits: one for every percent past 10. Bits buy the Bit tree, which lasts through everything, and every Bit you earn adds 2% ink for good. Tools you unlocked stay yours.

Select All unlocks the first time you reveal 90% of a picture. Once that picture's Dead Pixel is gone, it clears the rest in one sweep. Then Restore the picture for 90 Bits, its perk, and the next file in the folder.

A new picture is harder than the last. If you stall before Defrag opens, switch to a picture you already restored from the Gallery and replay it for Bits. Leaving a run early pays nothing, but your Bits and tools stay.
